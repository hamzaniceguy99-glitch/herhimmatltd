What we do, in detail

Four habits, each about being accurate rather than persuasive.

Program 01 · Checked, not copied

Verified composition

Reprinting a supplier's composition makes that statement yours, and if it is wrong the complaint and the offence are yours too. So we verify, and where we cannot verify something we either say so in the listing or do not stock the item. It is unglamorous work that nobody asks about, and it is the part of retail that actually has a legal standard.

  • Every listing
  • All customers
  • Verified or stated
  • Origin accurate

Program 02 · Size labels are not sizes

Real measurements

There is no standard behind a UK dress size, so a 12 from one brand and a 12 from another can differ by inches. Publishing the garment's own measurements lets you compare against something you already own, which is the only reliable method. We will also tell you when a cut runs small rather than letting you find out.

  • All customers

Program 03 · Which part, exactly

Claims we can evidence

A garment is rarely wholly anything. Organic cotton sewn with polyester thread, a recycled outer with a conventional lining. Where we make a claim we say which element it covers and name the scheme if one is relied upon. Where we cannot evidence it, we do not use the word, which makes our listings less reassuring and more accurate.

  • Where applicable
  • All customers
  • Evidence held
  • Scheme named

Program 04 · Including the exception

Returns explained first

You get fourteen days to cancel an online order without a reason. The exception people hit is sealed underwear and swimwear, which cannot come back once unsealed. We say that before you order rather than in a dispute afterwards, and we keep it separate from your rights if something is faulty, which are not time-limited in the same way.
